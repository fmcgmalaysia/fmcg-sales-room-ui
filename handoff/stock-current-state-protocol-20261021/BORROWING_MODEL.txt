Implemented behavior
Approve a transfer of lender-owned usable physical claims while reserving a specific
borrower PO source's unreceived capacity. Matching company/barcode/packing/ea, distinct
customer/task/source identities, positive quantity and approval evidence required.
Proof input assumes trusted complete source holds and valid PO authority. Strings such
as approvedBy and booleans such as complete/poValid are NOT real authorization.
Caller must execute existing employee, PO, invoice, packing and stock validators.
Borrower having a PO alone is insufficient: uncommitted future capacity and uncovered
customer demand both cap approval. Source onHand and original receipt/planned counters
do not change when ownership transfers. Source identities survive same-number PO.

Receipt is cumulative accepted quantity. It first repays bound relationships in approval
sequence, then fills owner demand after all-source dispatch/current claims/other incoming
replacement promises. Residual legitimate accepted stock remains available. Rejected or
isolated cartons do not enter this accepted-receipt transition. Unapproved overdelivery
rejected, not inventoried. Correction is deliberately not implemented here; downward
cumulative values reject rather than invent inverse allocation after dispatch.
The source acceptance update, claims and repayment changes are ONE pure after-state.
Production must translate that state into ONE existing fenced commit with the original
receipt event; separate commits/second receive endpoint are prohibited.

Dispatch preserves source-to-customer provenance and does not clear outstanding transfer.
taskCoverage exposes quantity completion separately from transfer obligations, never sets
the shared archived flag or bypasses existing invoice/financial completion requirements.
cumulativeTargets preserves shipped quantities in legacy lifetime allocations. Current
remaining claims alone cannot replace plannerAllocation.targets.

Guards/limits
Existing source-confirmed hold gap remains: packingListGuards currently has task-total
holds, not a universally verified source split. Model assumes complete trusted source
hold evidence and blocks borrower-held changes; it is not an implemented hold resolver.
Do not wire to production by relabelling task-total holds or passing browser assertions.
No indefinite borrowing chains of unreceived entitlements: donor must own actual source
claim; already borrowed claims cannot themselves serve as a new donor-owned source.
Outstanding relationship coverage blocks silent lender demand cancellation or borrower
PO capacity reduction. Explicit cancellation/reassignment/correction operations, immutable
authorization evidence, live row mapping and automatic-receipt integration remain pending.
Pure expectedRevision is NOT database concurrency control. Existing conditional commit
and durable same-request result replay remain mandatory; cumulative receipt replay only
proves quantity idempotence, and pure revision may advance on zero-delta receipt.
