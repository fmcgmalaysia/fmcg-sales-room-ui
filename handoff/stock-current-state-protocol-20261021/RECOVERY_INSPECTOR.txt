recovery-inspector.cjs accepts only store.read capability and exact original company/
request/member/input. Computes stable existing protocol identities/digest; reads control,
lock, PREPARED, RESULT and bounded exact row images, then rechecks BOTH guard records.
Matches original30write/180000byte proof bounds, collection/control reserved-ID allowlist,
exact prepared after-version hash and before-version separation. Does not fetch full
project, scan history, write, remove, unlock, force, initialize, retry or generate requests.
Normal budget at most36exact reads (4identities +30changes +2guard rechecks); older final
result after later committed generations skips obsolete business-image rereads. This is
algorithmic upper bound for this proof size, not Wix latency/production capacity claim.

Classifications
- No receipt: NOT CONFIRMED, not proof save failed or stock zero.
- Any surviving lock: stay blocked irrespective of age or matching owner. Changed guard:
  recheck later. Unknown read/network error propagates unchanged.
- Exact pending request/prepared plan, only before/after images: same-request resume.
- RESULT with exact pending/all after images: same-request finalize control only.
- Valid RESULT/generation: confirmed. Later legitimate operations may change current
  images; do not falsely flag an old completed save as broken or reread its old payload.
- Foreign pending: other operation pending. Wrong digest/company/identity, invalid plan/
  version/result, image conflict, missing barrier or missing prepared: review, no repair.
Output contains identities, generations/versions/attempt, presence flags and per-row
BEFORE/AFTER/CONFLICT classification plus fingerprint. NO input/plan/business row body.
Incident output remains private; never publish actual packets to this PUBLIC repo/Pages.
